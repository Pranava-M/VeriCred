How VeriCred Works

A verification layer for gig-worker loan requests: instead of a salary slip, six months of platform work history is scored, blended with CIBIL and bank balance, and recorded immutably on-chain — the final call always stays with the bank's people.

01
Work History
6 months of ride/delivery data — rides, ratings, cancellations, on-time %, payment mode.
02
AI Scoring
A weighted formula converts that history into a 0–100 trust score.
03
Oracle Bridge
The verified score is pushed on-chain by a trusted oracle process.
04
Blended Risk Meter
AI score + CIBIL + bank balance combine into one Risk Meter reading.
05
Officer Decision
The bank officer reviews the meter and forwards or declines — the system never auto-decides.
System Rules

1. The Risk Meter is not a decision

The system only ever shows a 0–100 Risk Meter reading (higher = safer, colour-coded green/amber/red). It never labels a request "approved" or "rejected" — that judgment call belongs to the bank officer, and ultimately the Bank Manager.

2. Three independent signals, blended

The Risk Meter blends the AI work-history score (55%), the CIBIL score (30%), and bank balance adequacy against the requested amount (15%). No single signal can carry a decision on its own — a strong work-history score cannot fully offset a very poor CIBIL file, and vice versa.

3. Editing the requested amount recalculates everything live

Because the balance-adequacy component and the EMI/DTI figures both depend on the requested amount, changing it immediately updates the Risk Meter, EMI, debt-to-income ratio, and system-suggested maximum — no page reload needed.

4. Every decision is recorded immutably

Forwarding a request to the Bank Manager, or declining it at officer level, writes a permanent, hash-linked block to the ledger — worker ID, score, risk reading, requested amount, and reviewing officer. Blocks are chained by SHA-256 hash, so any attempt to alter a past record breaks the chain and is immediately detectable on the Blockchain Ledger page.

5. The bank officer does not have final authority

Approving at officer level only forwards the request to the Bank Manager for final sign-off — mirroring how real loan desks operate. Declining at officer level stops the request at this stage. Both actions are logged the same way, permanently.

6. All applicant data on this demo is fictional

Names, ID numbers, bank account details, and ride histories used in this system are synthetic, generated for demonstration purposes only. No real individuals or accounts are represented.
